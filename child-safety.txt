Child safety standards
Yaarcast by Taskhive Studio
Effective 13 October 2026

Taskhive Studio prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion and sexualization of minors. These rules apply to profiles, posts, replies, communities, files and private messages.

Use the in-app report controls or email ratulkoley657@gmail.com with the subject Yaarcast child safety. Include the account handle or content reference; do not download, forward or attach suspected illegal imagery. This email is also the designated child-safety contact for authorities and platform inquiries.

We review reports, remove confirmed prohibited content and restrict offending accounts. Where required, we report confirmed CSAM to the appropriate regional authority or designated reporting organization and cooperate with lawful investigations. We do not offer automated detection or round-the-clock moderation in this beta.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. A parent/guardian can request assistance with an underage account through our support email. Never share passwords or unnecessary identity documents in a report.
